## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Explicit mindmap publishing
The system SHALL support a "Push to Students" action for mindmaps, allowing teachers to explicitly determine when a mindmap becomes visible to students.

#### Scenario: Pushing a mindmap
- **WHEN** Teacher clicks the "Push to Students" button on a mindmap
- **THEN** The mindmap's visibility flag updates to published, allowing students of the class to see it
