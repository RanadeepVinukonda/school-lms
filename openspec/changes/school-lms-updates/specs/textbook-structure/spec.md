## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Simplified concept pages
The textbook concept structure SHALL contain only three sections: Lecture Page, Notes & Resources, and Mindmap. Unused sections (Question Bank, Visibility, Push settings) MUST be removed from the UI.

#### Scenario: Viewing a concept
- **WHEN** User opens a concept page
- **THEN** The system shows tabs or pages only for Lecture, Notes & Resources, and Mindmap, with no visibility options
