## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Interactive learning modules for pre-primary grades
The system SHALL provide interactive learning modules for Pre-Nursery, Nursery, LKG, and UKG covering: rhymes (audio+animation), stories (illustrated), flash cards, tracing activities, phonics, and numeracy exercises.

#### Scenario: Child completes a tracing activity
- **WHEN** a UKG student opens a letter tracing activity
- **THEN** they SHALL be able to trace the letter using touch or mouse
- **THEN** on successful tracing, an encouraging animation and sound SHALL play
- **THEN** progress SHALL be recorded and visible to parents

### Requirement: Age-appropriate content safety
All pre-primary content SHALL be free of text complexity above a Grade 1 reading level. Instructions SHALL use audio narration in addition to text. Content SHALL meet COPPA/child safety standards.

#### Scenario: Audio instructions accompany every activity
- **WHEN** a Nursery student opens any activity
- **THEN** audio instructions SHALL play automatically
- **THEN** all buttons SHALL have icon labels in addition to text

### Requirement: Parent visibility into pre-primary progress
Parents SHALL be able to view their child's completed activities, time spent, and skill areas (alphabets, numbers, shapes, colors) mastery from the parent dashboard.

#### Scenario: Parent sees child's alphabet progress
- **WHEN** a parent views their child's progress
- **THEN** they SHALL see which letters have been traced successfully
- **THEN** they SHALL see the number of attempts and completion date for each letter
