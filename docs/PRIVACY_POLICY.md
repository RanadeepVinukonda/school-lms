## Data Sharing
We share data only with:
- **Cloud providers**: Supabase (database), Cloudinary (media storage)
- **AI providers**: Google Gemini, OpenRouter (textbook processing)
- **No third-party advertising**: We never sell personal data

## Your Rights
Under GDPR, you have:
1. **Right to access**: Export all your data via `/user/export`
2. **Right to rectification**: Update profile via settings
3. **Right to erasure**: Delete account via `/user/account` (30-day grace)
4. **Right to restrict processing**: Contact privacy@school-lms.com
5. **Right to data portability**: JSON export available
6. **Right to object**: Opt out of non-essential processing

## Security Measures
- Encryption at rest (database) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Pseudonymization where possible
- Access controls: role-based + school-scoped
- Regular security audits
